Who Are We

Two friends that love taking pictures

We love taking pictures not because we are professionals, but because every picture has a meaning to us. A photo can capture a moment, a feeling, or something beautiful that we saw and wanted to remember. You don’t have to be a professional photographer to enjoy photography.

You can take pictures however you want, in your own style, and simply enjoy the moment. Photography doesn’t always have to become a career, sometimes it can just be something we love.
