The people behind the camera

Hey, I'm Kai Yang, and I really like taking pictures.

I never really liked taking pictures before. Whenever I travelled to different places, I would rarely take photos because I believed that I should simply experience the moment with my own eyes and keep the memories in my mind. However, after a while, I began to feel that something was missing. When I wanted to share the places I had visited with my friends and family, I often found myself relying on photos from Google. Although those photos showed the beauty of the places, they were not from my perspective or a reflection of how I actually experienced them. I realised that I did not have a collection of photos that truly captured my own memories.

I wanted something more meaningful to look back on photos that captured not just the places I visited, but also the way I saw and experienced them. I wanted to share these moments with others and allow them to see the world through my perspective.

Hey, I'm Zi Yu, and I really like taking pictures too.

My journey with photography started with something simple a normal phone and the habit of taking pictures of anything. At first, I never thought much about it. I was just taking photos of places, people, and little moments that I found beautiful.

But one day, I saw people using cameras to capture moments that meant something deeply to them. It made me realise that a photograph is more than just an image. It can hold a memory, a feeling, and a moment in time that we may never experience in exactly the same way again. That inspired me to start capturing my own moments the happiness, the peace, the excitement, the quiet moments, and even the little things that others might not notice.
