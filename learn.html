Tips & tricks from us

Learning Photography

A few basics that helped us take better pictures.

Color Theory

Colors sitting opposite each other on the color wheel, like orange and blue, create contrast which makes your subject be more defined.

Red · Warm & bold
Orange · Warm & inviting
Green · Calm & natural
Blue · Cool & distant

Focusing

Tap or select your subject before you take a picture. A photo with a soft background and a defined subject almost always reads as intentional.

The Grid

Turn on your camera's grid and place your subject along one of the lines or at an intersection, instead of the center.
